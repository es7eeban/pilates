## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene yet commanding energy of a modern boutique Pilates and somatic wellness atelier. It unites the grounding, tranquil spirit of botanical wellness with the sophisticated polish of high-fashion editorial publishing. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Designed for discerning wellness enthusiasts, elite private practitioners, and multi-location studio directors who value precision, clarity, and aesthetic distinction. The interface evokes composure, bodily alignment, and quiet luxury—instantly eliminating the transactional tension typical of class booking platforms.

### Design Style: Modern Editorial Wellness
The visual direction balances refined editorial restraint with crisp contemporary SaaS utility:
- **Warm, Canvas-like Surfaces:** Natural off-white and soft porcelain backgrounds replace sterile digital white, evoking textured linen and sunlit architectural concrete.
- **Deep Plum & Vibrant Fuchsia Accents:** Deep regal plum anchors the structure, while vibrant fuchsia acts as an intentional energetic surge for high-value conversions and active states.
- **Botanical Sage Sub-tones:** Soft eucalyptus and mint hues introduce biological calm, communicating balance, capacity, and wellness indicators.
- **Precise Micro-Density:** Cards and modular chips prioritize rich legibility, neat metadata stacks, and compact utility, preserving generous negative space around macro layouts while keeping operational widgets impeccably dense.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate chromatic balance: rooted plum tones for typography and anchor actions, vivid magenta/fuchsia for conversion highlights, and herbal sage for holistic freshness.

### Roles & Application

- **Primary (`#3A1B38` - Deep Imperial Plum):** The brand's foundational tone. Used for dominant headings, high-priority interactive buttons, active tab indicators, and structured navigation icons. Replaces harsh true black to maintain warmth and luxury.
- **Secondary (`#D1286B` - Vivid Fuchsia Bloom):** Used selectively for urgency accents, "Last 2 Slots" urgency alerts, active booking states, promotional badges, and focal interactive hovers.
- **Tertiary (`#4D785D` - Botanical Sage & Mint):** Evokes natural calm. Deployed across capacity chips (e.g., "8 of 8 spots left"), status confirmations, verified instructor badges, and serene lifestyle iconography.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F8F5` - Alabaster Cream):** The ambient root canvas. Replaces standard cold paper white, infusing organic softness across background views.
- **Surface Cards & Modals (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure elevated white surfaces providing clean contrast above the creamy background.
- **Borders & Subtle Dividers (`#E8E5DF`):** Gentle stone outlines creating separation without visual weight.
- **Muted Neutral Text (`#6E6872`):** De-emphasized metadata, secondary timestamps, and instructor qualifications.

## Typography

The type system blends the character-rich, architectural structure of **Epilogue** for display headers with the balanced, highly readable geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, session timetables, and operational badges.

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Main Sections:** Hero announcements, live agenda titles, and studio headings render in `Epilogue` with tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`), giving the studio schedule an editorial, curated magazine look.
- **Card Titles & Class Types:** Class titles (e.g., *Reformer Sculpt & Alignment*) use `headline-sm` with a semi-bold weight to command attention before secondary details.
- **Metadata & Administrative Chips:** Timing, instructor profiles, studio room slots, and venue locations are displayed in `Plus Jakarta Sans` using `label-md` or `label-sm` with slight tracking expansion to maintain clarity in compact UI cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs spacious section architecture with a dense, scan-friendly grid for session schedules and venue selectors.

### Responsive Grid System
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Class schedule displays in a 3-column configuration (`col-span-4`), accommodating dense session cards side-by-side with unobstructed clarity.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. Class schedules reflow to a 2-column layout (`col-span-4`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Cards stretch across all 4 columns, with horizontal touch-scrolling for venue chips and discipline filters.

### Density & Rhythms
- **Compact Card Modules:** Card internal vertical flow follows an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` for related lines, `space-md` for distinct card zones: Header/Pills → Discipline Title → Details list → Price/CTA bar).
- **Navigation Shell:** Supports both a collapsible fixed left-rail sidebar for studio administration and a sleek, low-profile top bar with off-canvas hamburger drawer navigation on user-facing booking screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-opacity ambient shadows paired with soft stone surface borders, avoiding harsh corporate drops and preserving a soft, modern studio aesthetic.

### Depth System

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Used for base page canvas (`#F9F8F5`). No shadow or border.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Rest Surfaces):** Used for standard booking cards, studio selector pills, and segmented bar options. Features a micro-border `1px solid #ECE8E1` and an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(58, 27, 56, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Selected Cards):** When hovering over a class slot card or activating a venue selector. Elevates via: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(58, 27, 56, 0.08)`, border transition to `#D9D2C9`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Drawers & Modal Sheets):** For sliding sidebar menus, checkout booking sheets, and filter popovers. Features backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and a deep ambient drop: `0px 20px 40px -10px rgba(58, 27, 56, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced rounded architecture (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the smooth, continuous flow of Pilates movement without descending into juvenile pill shapes.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard Cards & Schedule Modules:** Constructed with `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii, framing dense information with welcoming curves.
- **Interactive Action Buttons & Form Controls:** Constructed with `0.5rem` (`rounded`) for a crisp, purposeful, and reassuring touch surface.
- **Badges, Status Chips & Venue Pills:** Use full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between actionable buttons and passive status tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Booking CTA ("Reservar Cupo"):** Deep imperial plum (`#3A1B38`) background with crisp white typography. Hover states subtly lift the button and introduce a plum tint overlay (`#4F244C`). Active click state shifts downward by 1px.
- **Secondary / Urgent CTA:** Vibrant fuchsia (`#D1286B`) background for high-priority bookings or final available spot confirmations.
- **Ghost & Outlined Buttons:** `1px solid #E2DED7`, soft plum text, transitioning on hover to a subtle cream tint (`#F1EFEA`).

### Badges & Capacity Chips
- **Available Spots Chip:** Mint/sage background (`#EBF3EE`) with deep sage text (`#2D523A`). Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padded with `6px 12px`, paired with a micro dot or counter icon (`e.g., 8 de 8 cupos`).
- **Urgent Availability Chip:** Soft peach-magenta container (`#FDF0F4`) with vibrant fuchsia text (`#D1286B`) for notifications like `¡Últimos 2 cupos!`.
- **Studio Venue Badge:** Muted stone-slate container (`#F3F1EC`) with plum-gray typography (`#5C5360`) and location map-pin icon.

### Cards (Class Session Slots)
- Structured into three distinct vertical zones within a single `rounded-lg` container:
  1. *Header Utility Row:* Venue badge (left) paired horizontally with the capacity chip (right).
  2. *Editorial Detail Core:* Date eyebrow, followed by bold class title (`Epilogue`, 18px), a 2-line snippet of class intensity/equipment, and icon-driven metadata items (Time, Duration, Instructor, Level).
  3. *Action Baseline:* Borderless bottom rack hosting the clear session price (`Epilogue`, 16px, bold) and the dedicated "Reservar Cupo" CTA button.

### Filter Bars & Venue Selectors
- **Venue Segmented Selector:** Contained horizontal strip with light stone border. Inactive venues feature clean typography with an outline star toggle; active venues switch to deep plum background with inverted white text.
- **Dropdown Filters (Disciplines, Schedules, Instructors):** Minimalist select inputs bounded by a `1px solid #E2DED7` border, rounded corners, chevron-down micro-icon, and subtle hover state transitions.

### Navigation Sidebar (Collapsible & Drawer)
- Clean off-canvas collapsible panel. Uses semi-translucent alabaster backing (`rgba(249, 248, 245, 0.95)` with backdrop blur). Menu items use deep plum text, with active routes highlighted by a subtle sage or fuchsia left indicator line.
